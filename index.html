Welcome to the Autistic Alliance for Advocacy

Coordinating and amplifying advocacy for autism and neurodivergence

Our Mission

We are a collective of advocates working together to create positive change for autistic and neurodivergent individuals. Our goal is to coordinate efforts, share resources, and amplify our voices in the pursuit of better understanding, acceptance, and support.

Join Our Weekly Meetings

We host weekly meetings where we discuss current advocacy efforts, share experiences, and plan future initiatives. Everyone is welcome to join and contribute to our cause.
